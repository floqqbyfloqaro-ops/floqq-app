# Meeting points: on-site verification checklist

A visit to Barcelona-El Prat to verify the nine candidate meeting points (five in T1, four in
T2B) with the admin capture tool. A point can only be used for groups once it has coordinates, a
photo, wording in English, French and Spanish, and has been marked as verified on site.

As a rough guide, allow about 2 hours per terminal. T1 and T2 are kilometres apart: the airport
runs a shuttle bus between them - check its current times before you go.

## Before you go

- [ ] Phone charged, with a power bank. The GPS, camera and screen will be on most of the time.
- [ ] Mobile data works at the airport (the app saves straight to the server; nothing is kept on
      the phone if the connection drops).
- [ ] The current development build is installed and opens.
- [ ] Signed in with the admin account. **Admin -> Points** lists nine points, all inactive.
- [ ] Location and camera are allowed for FLOQQ in the phone's settings, with **Precise Location**
      on (iPhone).
- [ ] A second phone with a passenger account, for the checks at the end.
- [ ] This checklist, printed or on a second device.

## At each point

Do these in order. The app only lets you verify once the first three are in, and only lets you
activate once it is verified.

1. **Walk there as a passenger would.** Start at the baggage claim exit of that terminal and walk
   to the point without using the app. Note how long it takes.
2. **Judge the spot.**
   - [ ] You can see the landmark from a distance, also when the hall is busy.
   - [ ] Three people with suitcases can stand there without blocking a door, a queue or a walkway.
   - [ ] It is in the public arrivals area: after baggage claim, before the exit, no ticket needed.
   - If the spot fails any of these, leave the point inactive and write down why.
3. **Capture the location.** Stand exactly where the group should stand. Open the point and tap
   **Use my current location**.
   - [ ] Note the accuracy shown. 15 m or better: tap **Save this location**.
   - [ ] Worse than 15 m: tap **Retake my location** once or twice, a few steps from walls and
         pillars. Still poor: switch the map to **Satellite**, long-press where you are standing,
         drag the pin if needed, then save.
   - [ ] On the map, the saved pin is inside the right building and on the right side of the hall.
4. **Take the photo.** Stand where a passenger first sees the landmark coming from the baggage
   claim exit, and photograph it from there.
   - [ ] The landmark's name or sign is readable in the photo.
   - [ ] No close-up faces of other travellers.
5. **Check the wording.** Read the name and the directions in all three languages while looking at
   the real place.
   - [ ] The directions start from the baggage claim exit and say left, right or straight ahead.
   - [ ] They name what a passenger actually sees (the sign as it is written, the level).
   - [ ] They are short enough to read while walking.
   - [ ] Corrected where needed, in all three languages, and saved.
6. **Set the priority.** Higher numbers are picked first. Give the easiest point in the terminal
   the highest number.
7. **Mark as verified on site.** Only when the pin, the photo and the wording are all right.
8. **Activate.** The list should now show the point as active with today's date.

If you change the location or the photo afterwards, the point loses its verification and is
deactivated: verify and activate it again before you leave the spot.

- [ ] At least two points are active, so two groups meeting at the same time get different points.
- [ ] Priorities reflect which points are easiest to find from the baggage claim exit.

- [ ] At least two points are active.

## While you are there

These need the real building, so they are worth doing on the same visit.

### The passenger's view

- [ ] With the passenger phone, create a ride without a flight number while standing in the
      terminal. The **Arrival terminal** is prefilled with the terminal you are in, with the note
      "Prefilled from your location". (Only works once that terminal has a verified point.)
- [ ] Confirm a test group at this terminal. Within a minute the passenger's **Find your group**
      screen shows the meeting point card: terminal, photo, name, directions and meeting time.
- [ ] **Open in Google Maps** opens at the point. Note whether Google Maps shows the inside of the
      terminal at that spot.
- [ ] My Ride shows the **Meet at** line.
- [ ] Switch the passenger phone to French and to Spanish: the name and directions follow.

### Live location indoors

- [ ] Two passenger phones in one confirmed group, both on **Find your group**, both sharing.
- [ ] Each phone shows the other as "Sharing live location". Note the accuracy each phone reports
      for itself in the arrivals hall: ______ m and ______ m.
- [ ] Walk apart to opposite ends of the hall and back. Note anything odd (a position that jumps,
      a phone that stops updating).
- [ ] Lock one phone: the other shows "Last seen" within a few seconds. Unlock it: sharing resumes.

Indoor accuracy is the main thing to learn here: it decides how close together the arrow screen
can still tell people apart.

## After the visit

- [ ] **Admin -> Points**: every point you want in use is active and shows today's verification
      date; the others are inactive.
- [ ] Points you rejected: note the reason in the table above, so they can be replaced.
- [ ] A new candidate you spotted on site can't be added from the app yet. Write down its name,
      terminal and where it is, and it will be added to the list.
